# Visual research notes

This scene was redesigned from recurring visual and experiential cues found across public Lahore travel documentation. No source photos or video are redistributed in the project.

## Recurring cues used

- Fort Road Food Street is defined by colorful heritage buildings, lamps, rooftop dining, food carts and the night view toward Badshahi Mosque and Lahore Fort.
- Visitor photography repeatedly shows turquoise, yellow, rust and red facades with wooden projected windows/balconies, dense signs, wires, lamps and small street counters.
- Street food references repeatedly mention BBQ/tikka, karahi, jalebi, kulfi, Kashmiri chai, sweets and anda-tikki style snacks.
- Recent reporting documents flower/tassel/truck-art decorated cycle/Rangeela rickshaws moving through the narrow lane past tea stalls, dessert vendors and puppet-show stands.
- Public heritage programming around the Food Street includes dhol, classical music, qawwali/ghazal-style cultural nights, costumed performers and traditional refreshments.
- A real Shahi Paan shop appears on Fort Road Food Street and is represented as a small red kiosk with a bright green sign.
- Roshnai Gate opens toward Fort Road Food Street; Hazuri Bagh, Iqbal's Tomb, Badshahi Mosque and Lahore Fort form the immediate heritage cluster beyond it.
